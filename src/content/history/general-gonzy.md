---
title: General Gonzy
order: 2
---

After the retirement of M1 a new sovereign had to be chosen, the populace (incited by a world-wide fever of war) thought general Gonzy was the right leader.

During Gonzy's term Merollissima continued growing in influence and economically via the construction of the east part of the city, becoming even the main influence of the Trittica village which later became its current colony.

Gonzy was a decent leader after all, maybe a little too harsh and autocratic but still the same empire M1 once dreamed about.

During the general Gonzy mandate the first world war broke out, Merollissima led the alliance with Polletto Town, The Borgo and the king of Bastiglia emilio-tan, sadly the rising sun coalition could not prevent the fall of the capital.

Gonzy was never found, some rumors say that he died during the attack of simomine’s empire. Merollissima had to concede political independence to the Trittica village, which concentrated its power into the Dragonspiral Tower.
