title: The Founding
order: 1
---

The Merollissima’s republic was born on the fourteenth day of the sacred Adrenalopolys council, since M1, valex11, Emilio the best were looking for an outpost to start a new civilisation after the end of the last server. M1 the Merollissima’s founder fell in love with the valley of snow nestled in the mountain, nearby a village that later became 'La Trittica'. M1, as thoughtful as strong, founded an empire with a premise: a Republic that chose democratically a doge for a lifetime, a figure that must fight for everyone's interests, protect every citizen and administrate the republic with long term plans and absolute power. Merollissima's first years of development were around the north zone and the Floating house supported by the 4 bridges symbols of the territory itself, trading amethyst mined locally with all the server, and serving as the first global economy center of the server (one of the first builds was the hall!), a tradition that until now has never stopped, but it has been amplified to other domains, since today Merollissima still trades rare materials such as crying obsidian, glass, all End world products, redstone, rare flowers, enchanted books, beacons and emeralds.
