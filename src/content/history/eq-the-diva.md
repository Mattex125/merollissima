title: EQ the Diva
order: 3
---

EQ the diva was the next leader to rise: a performative leader full of promises who was always quick to forget, a self-centered individual that’s truly unable to own up to one's faults. Merollissima during her term built the biggest collections of animals in the server on the west, founded a new colony: the turtles city where production of turtle’s derivatives, tridents became an important part of Merollissima’s economy, whose land was also used to build the first data center of the entire server. During EQ’s mandate, from The Amethyst Athenaeum an intellectual figure started to gain fame: Mattex125, a Phd under Cavaliere that resembled M1’s figure. Later on Mattex125 became the Mayor of the Trittica; with the populace within Merollissima craving a change and a return to their roots, coupled with EQ's inability to provide not gaslighting answers, it was inevitable that a civil war would break out. The Merollissima’s civil war was blood-soaked, a tragic page in the Republic's history books, but the 'Accademici' from Trittica, Polletto Town, and China Town were able to beat EQ’s army, the Jjunks Legion, and the Duchy of Creto. To celebrate the victory and the reunification between Trittica and Merollissima, the twin of the Dragonspiral Tower was inaugurated and a new era had begun.
