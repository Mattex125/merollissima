With Mattex being the central authority, Merollissima becomes once again the beacon empire that sets the standard for all to follow, reclaiming its central place on the global political stage, building fundamental infrastructure (Server’s Freeway, amethyst tree) and finally setting borders forging the south part of the republic, since lots of civilisations started to grow around this unique realm. And this is where our history meets the present day. The dark times are behind us, and a new, glorious era is unfolding right now.
