---
title: The portal of La Merollissima is inaugurated
date: 2026-10-18
summary: The Republic opens its official portal, with the Gazette, its History and a map of its builds.
---

Today the Republic of La Merollissima inaugurates its official portal.

From here, citizens and visitors can follow the life of the Republic:

- the **Gazette**, the official record of public works, notices and communications, numbered in
  order of publication;
- the **History** of the Republic, from its founding to the present day;
- the **Builds**, an interactive map of the territory and of its works.

The Gazette begins with this entry. Every future announcement of the Republic will be published
here.
